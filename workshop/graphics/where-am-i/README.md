# Where Am I?

A short visual transition from darkness into a real astronomical image.

The sequence begins with the human question:

> Where am I?

The question fades as the Hubble Ultra Deep Field appears.

This image does not show the entire universe. It shows a small region of
sky containing thousands of galaxies and serves as a truthful visual
representation of the larger cosmos in which we exist.
